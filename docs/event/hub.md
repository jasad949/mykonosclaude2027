## The problem in one line (hub, section 03)

> In Orange County a median home costs about $1.47 million, and only about 15% of households can
> afford one (C.A.R., Q3 2026). It takes about four years from finding land to handing over keys,
> and the cost side usually wins by six figures a home.
>
> The family's share is fixed by income. Lanes 1 and 2 shrink the cost; Lane 3 closes the gap.
>
> What the family can pay + public subsidies + fundraising − the cost to create the home = $0

| Lane | Question | Hub prompt | Who's stuck |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Find it | Where can the next affordable home go? | The laws that open up land are public. So is the parcel data. Why does finding a site still come down to luck, and what would change that? | A nonprofit acquisitions lead with no GIS team, a congregation with an underused lot, city housing staff |
| 2 Clear it | How do we turn four years into two? | The fast, ministerial path exists on paper. Why is it so hard to find and follow in practice, and what would make it the normal path? | A nonprofit project manager, a small builder, a homeowner adding an ADU, a city permit tech |
| 3 Fund it | Does it pencil, and who closes the gap? | Every project has a gap between cost and what the family can pay. What makes it smaller, or the money that closes it easier to find? | A nonprofit finance lead, a development director, a donor, a city housing analyst |

Any lane works in any room. Full lane pages are in [`lanes/`](../../lanes/).

## Submit (hub, section 04)

Due **2:30 PM**. One per team; any teammate with an email on the team can update it.

Have these ready:
- **Public repo** with a README. Start from [`templates/TEAM-README.md`](../../templates/TEAM-README.md).
  No API keys or passwords in it.
- **Demo video:** 30 seconds ideal, up to 2 minutes. Upload the file or paste a link.
- **One real user:** who it's for, before and after.
- **Sources:** laws, datasets, links. Label every number you assumed.

The form: find your team by any team email; details carry over. Full field list:
[`docs/submission.md`](../submission.md).

## Judging (hub, section 05)

Overall 1st, 2nd and 3rd across all three lanes (the lane repo also mentions a winner per lane).

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 2:30 PM | **Shortlist.** Judges watch every video and pick 10 finalists, the teams with the most judge votes |
| 3:00 PM | **Live demos.** Finalists demo live on the auditorium stage |
| 4:15 PM | **Winners.** Highest average across four equal areas. Ties go to Impact |

| Area (25% each) | Question |
|---|---|
| Impact | A nonprofit or city would use it next month to help get homes built |
| Works | Live demo on real data, sources shown. Rough and working beats polished |
| Scale | A believable path to the next 100 projects |
| Innovation | A new way to see or solve the problem. Bend the brief if it serves the goal |

Before you build:
- Start with one real person and the problem they're stuck on.
- Public or provided data only. Don't overload city websites. No private plans or personal
  information.
- Your team owns what you build. Public repo; an open license is encouraged.
- **Slides without a working demo do not place.**
