# 30-second video script

Record the running app (`bash scripts/run_demo.sh`, http://localhost:8501) with any screen recorder.
Narrate live or add captions. Target 30 s; hard limit 2 min. Script drafted with Claude.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Title + HISTORICAL badge | "Habitat OC is building six affordable homes in Santa Ana. They still have to raise money to finish." |
| 5–12 s | Needs column: $150,211 upper bound, $220,000 temporary | "Our tool splits the gap: up to $150,211 must be raised for good, and $220,000 is only a timing gap the city repays once every home closes." |
| 12–18 s | Hover the source chips (audit Note 7, city report, grant §4.2) | "Every number comes from a public audit, city report or council agreement, or it's marked assumption or unknown." |
| 18–25 s | Sidebar: foundation willing = yes, city consents = yes, deal cost $2,000 → WHAT-IF badge, verdict flips to the advance, net $2,950 | "Ask: gift plus our credit line, or gift plus a foundation advance? The advance only wins if it costs under $4,950 and the city allows repayment from its final payment." |
| 25–30 s | Click Draft proposal; show the evidence-still-needed list | "The finance lead leaves with a draft proposal and the four facts to confirm. Built with Claude." |

Keep on screen: the "Prototype, not financial advice" line at the top.
